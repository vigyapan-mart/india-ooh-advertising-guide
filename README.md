# India OOH Advertising Guide

Outdoor advertising (OOH) helps brands reach audiences through physical
advertising placements in public spaces, transit networks, and commercial areas.

## Types of Outdoor Advertising

- Billboard advertising
- Bus advertising
- Metro advertising
- Airport advertising
- Auto-rickshaw advertising
- Street furniture advertising
- Digital outdoor advertising (DOOH)
- Wall painting advertising

## How Brands Choose OOH Advertising

Campaign planning generally depends on:

- Target audience and demographics
- City and location selection
- Traffic and visibility
- Campaign duration
- Advertising format
- Budget and creative design

## Outdoor Advertising in India

India's outdoor advertising landscape includes roadside media,
transit advertising, retail media, digital screens and city-level
advertising placements.
